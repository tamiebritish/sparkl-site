Terms of Service

Last updated: January 2025

1. Platform role

Sparkl is a marketplace platform that connects clients with independent cleaning professionals in Hamilton, Ontario. Sparkl is not a cleaning company and does not employ cleaners. All cleaning services are provided by independent contractors.

2. Independent contractor relationship

Cleaners listed on Sparkl are self-employed independent contractors. They set their own rates, manage their own schedules, and are responsible for their own tax obligations. Sparkl facilitates discovery and booking but is not a party to the service agreement between cleaner and client.

3. Booking requests

Submitting a booking request does not guarantee a confirmed booking. Cleaners may accept or decline requests. Payment is collected only after a booking is confirmed by the cleaner.

4. Platform fee

Sparkl charges a service fee of 10–15% on each confirmed booking. This fee covers platform maintenance, trust and safety programs, and client support.

5. Cancellation

Clients may cancel a confirmed booking without penalty up to 24 hours before the scheduled start time. Late cancellations may incur a fee at the cleaner's discretion.

6. Off-platform arrangements

Arranging bookings outside of Sparkl voids all platform protections including dispute resolution, insurance coverage, and cleaner accountability measures. Both clients and cleaners agree not to transact off-platform.

7. Contact

Questions about these terms: hello@sparkl.ca

Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

1. What we collect

When you submit a booking request or cleaner application, we collect: your name, email address, phone number, and the details of your request. We do not sell this information to third parties.

2. How we use it

We use your information to facilitate bookings, send confirmation emails, and improve the platform. Cleaner profiles (name, bio, neighbourhood, services) are publicly visible on the platform.

3. Data storage

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, hosted in Canada. We retain booking records for up to 3 years for dispute resolution purposes.

4. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by emailing privacy@sparkl.ca.

5. Cookies

We use minimal session cookies required for platform functionality. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

Trust & Safety

Last updated: January 2025

Cleaner vetting

Every cleaner on Sparkl has completed an application process and been manually reviewed by our team before their profile is made public. We verify identity and conduct background checks where consented.

Quality standards

Cleaners must maintain a minimum average rating to remain listed. Profiles with consistent poor reviews or client complaints are suspended and reviewed.

Dispute resolution

If a cleaning does not meet expectations, contact us within 48 hours. We will work with both parties to reach a fair resolution, which may include a re-clean or partial refund.

Off-platform warning

We strongly advise against arranging bookings outside of Sparkl. Off-platform bookings are not covered by our dispute resolution process, and there is no accountability if something goes wrong.

Report a concern

To report a cleaner or a safety concern: safety@sparkl.ca
